-~=================================~-
    Kobold Transformation Toggle
-~=================================~-

This mod adds a simple toggle when ingame to change your player's skin to that of a kobold.
You can find this toggle ingame right next to where the pvp team icons would be found. Simply enabling the toggle will transform you

This mod and transformation works on servers, and other players should be able to see you as a kobold when transformed.
The mod will also save the choice of transformation to the player's save file.

Note: some human hair styles change to horns when transform!
